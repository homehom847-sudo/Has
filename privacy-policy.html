🔒 Privacy Policy

SUSU JAMB PREP respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

Your name, your aimed JAMB score, your practice history, and your progress data (such as streaks, XP, and accuracy by subject) are stored so the app can show your dashboard and track your improvement over time.

How it's stored

In this version of the app, your data is stored locally on your own device and is not sent to any external server.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Your choices

You can log out at any time from your Profile page, and you can clear your locally stored data through your browser's site settings.
